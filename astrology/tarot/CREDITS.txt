Tarot card art — Rider–Waite–Smith deck
========================================

Files: 0.jpg … 77.jpg, indexed to the app's card order (DATA.TAROT):
  0–21   Major Arcana (The Fool … The World)
  22–77  Minor Arcana (Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles — Ace … King)

Artwork: the Rider–Waite–Smith Tarot, illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith
(1878–1951), first published 1909. The original deck is in the public domain
in the United States and many other jurisdictions (published pre-1928; its
author died in 1951).

Source: the scans were obtained from the npm package
@cometpisces/tarot-kit-images (MIT-licensed code; the bundled images are the
public-domain RWS deck). They were recompressed here to small JPEGs (~300px
wide) purely to keep the offline bundle light.

These images are included so the Tarot screen works fully offline. When the
device is online, the app additionally flickers through other public-domain
versions of each card from Wikimedia Commons.
