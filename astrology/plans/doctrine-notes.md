# DOCTRINE NOTES
## The school's method, distilled from Sonia's two preparatory course documents

**Provenance and usage rule.** These notes distill the *method and facts* of
the two "Cours préparatoires" documents (Western traditional astrology,
London School of Astrology lineage; the course itself draws on Ibn Ezra,
Denis Labouré, and the classical authors). The course documents are
copyrighted teaching material: **nothing of their expression may be copied
into the app**. What follows is our own wording of the doctrine. Where the
course itself quotes a source, we cite the underlying source: Ibn Ezra
(12th c., public domain) may be quoted; Labouré (modern) is paraphrased
only. All prose that ships in the app is written fresh from these notes and
from the public-domain authors in `forty-phases.md`.

## 1. The frame (the whole reading rests on this)

- **Houses are life-domains.** Career, marriage, children, parents,
  siblings, money, illness, death, travel — the reading is about the
  *domains of a life*, not about a personality profile. (The modern
  reduction of astrology to the self is the school's core complaint.)
- **Planets are the actors.** Each has *natural effects* (what it does in
  every chart, everywhere) and *accidental effects* (what it does in THIS
  chart through the houses it rules and occupies — "accidental" =
  chart-particular, temporary).
- **Signs tell you the STATE of a domain; houses tell you the
  CIRCUMSTANCES.** This is the fundamental distinction. Zodiacal state
  (dignity/debility — the course calls it *état céleste*) says whether the
  thing is sound, strong, lasting, collapsing. Terrestrial state (house
  placement, angularity — *état terrestre*) says what *happens* to it, in
  what setting, with what force. A planet can be in fall (state: fragile)
  yet angular and famous (circumstances: prominent) — the reading must hold
  both without blending them.
- **Benefics favor the domain they touch; malefics disturb it.** Not the
  person — the domain. A malefic is not hostile and a benefic is not kind:
  each simply promotes or hinders *the matters of the house it touches*.
  (Saturn debilitated in the 10th is bad for the career — and welcome if
  you wanted early retirement.) The biological image: benefics carry
  moisture (growth), malefics carry dryness (withering).

## 2. Benefic / malefic, with the nuances the app must encode

| Body | Doctrine |
|---|---|
| Jupiter | Greater benefic (hot + moist — both growth qualities). |
| Venus | Benefic (cold + moist). |
| Moon | Benefic when LIGHT: more than 90° from the Sun (i.e., between 90° and 270° of elongation) and/or waxing. Malefic when dark: within 90° of the Sun; a waning Moon losing light is worse than a waxing one. |
| Mercury | Convertible/neutral. Takes the nature of what touches it: with benefics very benefic, with malefics very malefic. Alone, lean benefic — but its NEXT applying aspect shows how it will end up behaving. |
| Sun | Benefic alone and by aspect; strongly malefic by close conjunction — it burns what sits within **7°30′** (combustion). |
| Mars | Malefic (hot + dry): burns, breaks, ruptures; sudden and conflictual. |
| Saturn | Greater malefic (cold + dry — the anti-life pair): restricts, removes, condenses, separates, slows. |
| North Node | Amplifies whatever it joins — benefic OR malefic. Favors concrete affairs of its house, but can give and take back; hard on peace of mind. |
| South Node | Reduces whatever it joins — benefic OR malefic. Unfavorable for the concrete affairs of its house, yet gives gifts/talents there. |

Also: a diurnal planet in a diurnal sign (or nocturnal in nocturnal) is
always more helpful / less harmful than when the categories clash — this
concordance rule modifies *every* judgment of benefit and harm, including
for peregrine planets.

## 3. Essential dignity — five layers, with points AND distinct characters

Scoring (used for almutens and for the condition verdict):
domicile **+5**, exaltation **+4**, triplicity **+3**, term **+2**,
face/decan **+1**; fall **−4**, detriment/exile **−5**.

Each layer is not just a score — it has its own *narrative character*, and
the app's language must keep them distinct:

- **Domicile (+5)** — the ruler at home, free in its own house. Pure,
  constant action at full level. What it rules ARRIVES and LASTS; the
  domain is stable. Extra strength when the domicile's sect/element agrees
  with the planet's own.
- **Exaltation (+4)** — the honored guest: great power without the
  responsibilities of ownership, a touch of extravagance. The thing comes
  SUDDENLY, in bursts, with high impact and high rank — but the position
  is subject to tribulations (especially under bad aspects from the
  malefics). Power increases; *quality* does not.
- **Triplicity (+3)** — a man among his family (Ibn Ezra): comfortable,
  regular, balanced, honored, though not master of the house. Its signature
  outcome: the native breaks through **by the help of other people** —
  advice, intercession, backing. The Dorothean day/night lords apply
  (day chart → day lord in triplicity, etc.).
- **Term (+2)** — a man in his own seat (Ibn Ezra): confined to a small
  space, but *tempered* — never excessive, never deficient. Terms are the
  **Egyptian** set only (Sonia is explicit); only the five non-luminaries
  hold terms — Sun, Moon and Nodes have none.
- **Face/decan (+1)** — a man dressed in his fine clothes, about to be put
  out the door: the weakest dignity, dignified in appearance, fragile in
  substance — credit and reputation hard to maintain.
- **Peregrine (0, no dignity at all)** — the vagabond: no title, no status
  in the territory it crosses. What it promises tends to come through
  strangers or unknown people; it lacks the power to deliver a *durable*
  benefit. It leans entirely on its dispositor — the dispositor's condition
  decides what the peregrine planet can finally give. A planet cannot be
  peregrine while holding any dignity; it CAN be peregrine and also in
  fall/detriment (worse).
- **Detriment/exile (−5)** — the worst state: the planet occupies enemy
  territory; its elemental nature is contradicted by the sign AND it is
  subordinated to a ruler whose agenda opposes its own. It acts without
  coordination, hindered from its proper work, weakened for good and
  strengthened for harm. What it rules does not last and finally collapses,
  becoming dependent on the dispositor — a benefic in exile still does some
  good, but less and with far more difficulty. Worse in the exile sign
  whose sect differs from the planet's own.
- **Fall (−4)** — collapse of force: the planet is a pretender who cannot
  deliver what it promises, and reacts to the humiliation by showing its
  faults more than its virtues. The thing does not stand; sooner or later
  it falls (timing decides when). The dispositor can aggravate or soften.

## 4. Accidental state

- **House category**: angular (I, IV, VII, X) strongly amplifies the
  occupant; succedent (II, V, VIII, XI) mildly amplifies; cadent (III, VI,
  IX, XII) weakens. A dignified planet cadent = good quality, less force
  (the course's example: exalted Sun in the 9th — fine state, muted
  delivery).
- **Combustion**: within 7°30′ of the Sun the planet is burned (the Sun
  acting as malefic). (Cazimi/under-beams refinements from the broader
  tradition slot in here.)
- **Feral (unaspected)**: a planet receiving no aspect acts alone — the
  person or matter it signifies is unsupported, isolated. (In the course's
  worked example, the feral Sun as the father's universal significator =
  the father has no backing.)

## 5. The almuten — "the true lord of the domain"

- For any point (a house cusp, a planet's degree), score every planet's
  dignities AT THAT DEGREE (domicile 5, exaltation 4, triplicity 3 by sect,
  Egyptian term 2, face 1). The highest scorer is the **almuten** — from
  Arabic, "the strongest, the victor" — and it can differ from the domicile
  ruler. Example logic: MC at 28° Aries — Mars has only domicile (5), but
  the Sun has exaltation (4) + day-triplicity (3) = 7, so the SUN judges
  the career.
- **Ties are content, not noise**: several almutens of one house = several
  of the thing (several illnesses in the 6th, several employers, several
  animals). Read what the sky shows.
- Beginners may read almuten alone; the full method reads BOTH almuten and
  domicile lord — two windows on the same domain.
- The same computation applies to **dispositors**: the dispositor of a
  planet may be taken by almuten of its degree, not merely by sign ruler.

## 6. Analogy — the selection key (this is how the composer CHOOSES)

A house rules many matters; a planet rules many matters. The reading is
decided by **analogy: the point in common between the planet and the house
it occupies (or rules, or aspects)**. Analogy is the PRIORITY rule — it is
what turns a list of significations into a specific statement. The 4th can
mean parents, home, endings, vehicles; the Sun in the 4th speaks FIRST of
the father, Jupiter in the 4th FIRST of family patrimony.

Sources of analogy, in the order the method checks them:

1. **Joys (Greek).** Each of the seven planets has a house where its first
   quality matches the domain and expresses openly, for good or ill:
   Mercury I (incarnation, the body-life link), Moon III (the restless
   mover — mind and movement), Venus V (pleasure, beauty, love's
   beginnings), Mars VI (sudden hurts — acute illness, accidents, enemies),
   Sun IX (God-light — religion, authentic spiritual experience),
   Jupiter XI (increase — helps, attainment of wishes),
   Saturn XII (the far, slow one — isolation, chronic illness). A planet
   in its joy makes its shared domain the loudest thing in the chart.
2. **Chaldean co-significators.** Lay the planets in Chaldean order
   (Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon) against houses
   I–XII, wrapping: Saturn co-signifies I (bones, skin) and VIII (death,
   the hidden), Jupiter II (abundance) and IX (the teacher/priest),
   Mars III (initiative, risk) and X (action, victory), Sun IV (the
   father) and XI (honors, the spotlight), Venus V (love, pleasure) and
   XII (rest, indulgence, its shame), Mercury VI (service, expertise),
   Moon VII (the spouse). A planet occupying its co-significator house
   speaks first of their shared matter.
3. **Universal significators (karakas).** Each planet rules certain
   matters in EVERY chart (Sun father/authority, Moon mother/people/
   body-changes, Venus love/women/art, Mars brothers/wounds/surgery,
   Mercury speech/trade/youth, Jupiter wealth/children/teachers, Saturn
   the old/land/chronic things). A planet in a house shared with its
   universal signification speaks of that first: Moon in IV = the mother;
   Jupiter in V = a child; Mars in III = a brother; Mars in X = the
   profession's conflicts and ambition.
4. **Indirect analogy by rulership.** The lord of a house carries that
   house's matter wherever it goes: Jupiter lord of V speaks first of the
   child; Saturn lord of VIII of the native's death; Jupiter lord of VIII
   of inheritance/spouse's money.
5. **Indirect analogy by aspect.** A planet aspecting the significator of
   a matter acquires a link to that matter (the Moon aspecting the lord
   of X also describes the mother/career).

**Counter-analogy.** When planet and house *contradict* (Jupiter, vital
protection, in the XII of chronic illness; Saturn, restriction, in the II
of substance), judge by the planet's benefic/malefic nature AND its state:
the benefic in a malefic house protects (illness comes but the native
escapes — the benign version of the thing); the malefic in a benefic house
hinders (Saturn in II withholds and separates the native from money —
UNLESS in good zodiacal state, when it can build slowly instead). As a
rule benefics are in counter-analogy in the malefic houses (VI, VIII,
XII), malefics in all the rest.

## 7. Cause and effect — the lord of X in house Y

The lord of a house placed in another house **connects the two domains,
one as cause of the other**. Lord of I in VIII: the native is bound to
investigation/death-matters. Lord of II in IX: money comes THROUGH the
9th's matters (teaching, abroad, spirituality). Lord of XI badly placed in
XII: friends end as enemies; lord of XII well placed in XI: hidden enemies
become friends. This is also the consultation tool: for money read lords
of II and XI and judge the houses they sit in ("lord of II in VII: income
through consultation, trade, partnership, marriage"); for marriage read
the lord of VII's house ("in X: love met at work or in public view; in
III: met online, in transit, through siblings/neighbors"). The same logic
runs through almutens and through conjunctions/aspects between lords
(almuten of III conjunct almuten of VII: the native travels to the
partner).

## 8. The zodiacal matrix (whole-sign destiny grid)

A sign is not a trait-list; it is a **territory** — understood by which
planets are at home, exalted, exiled and fallen there. Overlay whole-sign
houses from the Ascendant and read each house's sign through those four
natural tenants: that is the chart's *matrix*, the fate-skeleton every
person with that rising shares, BEFORE any planet is placed. (Leo rising:
the III falls in Libra = the Sun's fall + Saturn's exaltation + Mars's
exile + Venus's domicile → the sibling relationship is distant/opposite in
temperament, initiative easily discouraged, yet underlying goodwill and an
artistic vein.) Planetary configurations then override and specify; when a
configuration AGREES with the matrix, the indicated thing is larger and
more important in the life. The matrix is the confirmation layer, checked
LAST.

## 9. Aspects — pipe and liquid

- Natures: **sextile** is venusian (mild, harmonious), **trine** jupiterian
  (ease, growth, flourishing), **square** martial (conflict, effort),
  **opposition** saturnian (separation, rupture, distance),
  **conjunction** mercurial/neutral — a cohabitation that is collaboration
  between friends or confrontation between enemies.
- **The aspect tells you the state of the pipe (bent or straight); the
  planet tells you the liquid flowing through it (clean or foul).** Hence
  the school's axiom: *better a square of Jupiter than a trine of Saturn.*
  Jupiter acts jupiterially through any pipe (it grows what it touches —
  even its square mainly marks tension plus increase); Saturn acts
  saturnially through any pipe (even its trine smoothly delivers
  restriction and delay). The planet's nature outranks the aspect's
  geometry. Judgment always composes: aspect kind × both planets' natures
  × both states × what each rules in THIS chart.
- Direction matters: Jupiter's aspect TO Mars helps what Mars rules;
  Mars's aspect TO Jupiter heats/harms what Jupiter rules.

## 10. The complete method (the course's judgment algorithm)

For each concrete domain of life (finances, career, love, parents,
siblings, children, home, health…):

1. Ask the question: what does this domain promise? (the thread)
2. Find its house. Any occupant? Judge by the occupant's benefic/malefic
   nature.
3. Judge the occupant's ACCIDENTAL effects — what it rules in this chart
   arrives INTO this house.
4. Judge the occupant's zodiacal state (dignity/debility/neutral).
5. Find the LORD of the house; its zodiacal state gives the domain's
   final promise.
6. The lord's house and house-category give the circumstances.
7. Planets conjunct the lord modify it by their natures and by what they
   rule.
8. Check the universal significator (karaka) of the domain — its state
   and house.
9. Repeat per domain.
10. Then open judgment: benefics/malefics through the houses generally.
11. Throughout: ANALOGY selects which signification speaks.
12. Finish by touring the zodiac with all twelve house-lords.

And the analogy sub-method (from the second course document): planets in
joy first → planets in Chaldean co-signification → other analogies →
counter-analogies → dispositors (by almuten) → aspects → cause-and-effect
lord-links → confirm against the zodiacal matrix.

## 11. Three levels of reading (register doctrine for the voice book)

1. **Objective**: the cold technical statement of what stands in the chart
   — the description a scholar would defend.
2. **Inspired**: the synthesis where the description comes to life as a
   human story — warmth added AFTER the technique, never instead of it.
3. **Expert**: practical counsel, which the chart itself never gives —
   only an astrologer with real domain expertise may add it, clearly as
   advice, not as fate.

The app's registers map to: receipts = level 1; the composed reading =
level 2 grounded in level 1; level 3 we do NOT fake — no life advice
beyond what the configuration itself warrants.

## 12. What the worked examples teach about composition

The course's father-example is the model sentence-architecture the engine
must reproduce: *house occupant judged by nature and by what it rules*
("Jupiter in IV describes the father; but as lord of VIII he tends to
destroy what he touches — here, the father") → *lord's state gives the
promise* ("Venus, lady of IV, in exile: the father's condition poor, his
span likely shortened") → *aspects add circumstances* ("the Moon, ruling
and placed in XII, squares her: distance, separation from the father") →
*karaka confirms* ("the Sun, universal significator of fathers, is feral:
the father stands unsupported"). Four testimonies, one judgment, every
clause carrying a chart-specific determinant. That is the register bar
restated from the school's own practice.
