## PART A — PREDICTION BY SYMBOLIC DIRECTIONS (Cours 5)

### A1. The principle
- **Symbolic, not astronomical.** Advance any factor (a planet, the Ascendant, the
  MC, a Lot) by **1° per year of life** — the Sun's mean daily step read as a year.
- When the directed factor reaches another chart factor **by conjunction or
  aspect**, a **concrete event** occurs, dated by the number of degrees.
- A directed factor may contact: a **radical planet**, a **house cusp**, a **new
  sign** (as it ingresses), or a **Lot**. The movers may be: a planet, the Asc,
  the MC, a Lot.
- **Solar arc** is the same idea at the exact solar rate — more precise. Rule:
  symbolic (1°/yr) when quick; solar arc when refining. Precision ≈ ±1–2 years;
  the effect runs while the directed body stays within the 1° window of the
  radical degree.

### A2. The 30-second quick method
1. The **first planet to reach the Ascendant** by direction (esp. one in the XII /
   12th sign from the Asc → its house-symbolism colours the event).
2. The **first planet to reach the MC** (career/honours/visibility events).
3. The **Mars–Saturn square/opposition** (the two malefics — hard, unpleasant, and
   near angles/VIII can mark the end of life).

### A3. The interpretation rules (for serious astrologers)
- **Never alone.** Use ≥2 techniques (directions + profections + returns +
  transits) and make them *dialogue* until one hypothesis is confirmed.
- **Read what the mover RULES and OCCUPIES**, and the house it touches — you must
  know the house significations cold.
- **The natal planet's zodiacal state = does it last.** (Directed Sun conj Venus
  → success in art; Venus in exile → it did not last: the band broke up in 2 yrs.)
- **The aspecting planet's nature = the detail** (Sun as lord of I = the native
  himself, the chief → he was the frontman).
- **A direction gives only what the theme PROMISES.** Do a **static analysis of the
  domains first**; never predict a child/enemy the chart doesn't promise.
- **Occupancy tends to win over rulership** (Jupiter in IV → housing first, then
  child/loss as lord of V/XII) — but **analogy always primes**.
- **A directed planet changing SIGN is itself an event** (Saturn, lord of housing,
  ingressing → the housing/father's health declined).
- Transaturnians: judge them by **house position** (no reliable dignities).

### A4. THE DERIVED-HOUSES FAN-OUT — the narrative multiplier
Turn the chart so the house of a **person** becomes the Ascendant, and re-read the
SAME direction from their point of view. Derive from: **III sibling · IV father ·
V child · VI the animal · VII spouse · IX teacher · X mother · XI friend · XII
enemy.** One direction then yields **a whole set of dated events across the
native's entire cast** — the mother's health, the sibling's marriage, the father's
career — from a single motion. This is the engine of countless, time-based,
chart-specific narratives: every year, one arc fans into many.

### A5. The 10-step direction method
1 list the directions · 2 what each mover **occupies** · 3 what each **rules** ·
4 decide by **analogy** · 5 favourable/unfavourable (aspect + planet nature) ·
6 the **natal state** of the aspected planet (does it last) · 7 where the movers
**end up** (sign changes) · 8 list the **hypotheses** · 9 **confirm** with other
systems (returns, transits) · 10 **derive** the chart for the whole cast.

## PART B — MEDICAL ASTROLOGY (Cours 6)

Natal medical = temperament, the fragile zones, the promised afflictions of a
life. (Horary "décombiture" is out of scope for the app for now.)
**Reassure first:** the promises are potentials across a whole life, never all at
once. First find the promises; then **date the crises with directions / returns /
transits** (Part A + the timing engine we already have).

### B1. Sign → body zone (Lilly "L" / Sepharial "S")
Aries head/face · Taurus throat, neck, ears · Gemini arms, shoulders, hands ·
Cancer stomach, breast, chest, lungs · Leo heart, back, ribs · Virgo intestines,
belly · Libra kidneys, lower back, hips, joints · Scorpio genitals, anus, bladder ·
Sagittarius hips, thighs, buttocks · Capricorn knees, joints, skin, bones · Aquarius
legs, shins, calves, ankles · Pisces ankles, feet. (Plus internal-governance and
planet→body-part tables from the same sources.)

### B2. Planet → affliction type (Sepharial/Raphael)
Moon colds, damp, swellings, irregularities · Sun fevers, sunstroke, excess heat ·
Mercury nervous trouble, insomnia, mental strain, dietary irregularity · Venus
reproductive/skin, weakness, poison · Mars heat, inflammation, ruptures, wounds,
bleeding, fevers, acute pain, accidents · Jupiter apoplexy, pleurisy, corrupted
blood, diseases of excess · Saturn colds, obstruction, constipation, ossification,
latent & chronic, dull intense pain · (moderns: Uranus cardiac/stroke if to Sun,
Neptune psychosomatic/addiction, Pluto occupational/mines/radiation).
**Sun = chronic, organic, hereditary; Moon = acute, functional, acquired**
(from food, climate, habit). A benefic afflicts only by **hard aspect**; a malefic
by **conjunction, parallel or hard aspect**.

### B3. The method — life forces vs morbid forces
1. **Judge the forces of life** — the hylegiacal places: **Ascendant, its lord,
   Moon, Sun** (+ the Lot of life). Strong signs (Aries/Leo/Sag = vigour), robust
   (Gemini/Libra/Aqua), weak (Cancer/Scorpio/Pisces); odd signs stronger than
   even; luminaries above the horizon stronger.
2. **Judge the morbid forces** — the malefics Saturn & Mars, plus the accidental
   malefics: the **lords of VI, VIII, XII** (some add IV), and the Lot of sickness.
3. **Who is stronger** — life or morbidity.
4. **Are the life-forces afflicted** by the morbid ones (aspects to Sun/Moon/Asc).
5. **Locate the afflicted body zones** — the weakest planets' signs/houses, cross-
   confirmed: the **maximum of concordant testimonies** names the truly weak zone.
   VI, XII, their cusps, lords and occupants give the principal troubles; the sign
   ruled by an afflicting malefic names the part.

### B4. The Raphael planet-in-sign disease matrix (public domain)
Saturn/Jupiter/Mars × 12 signs → concrete diseases (e.g. Mars in Virgo: enteritis,
peritonitis, dysentery, hernia; Saturn in Pisces: gout, rheumatism, tender/flat
feet). A large authored lookup, sourced to Raphael — a storylet table for the
health domain, fired only where the planet actually afflicts a life-force.
