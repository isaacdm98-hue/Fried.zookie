# THE TRANSFORMATION — the app as a consultation, everywhere

The owner's brief, distilled from a day of the sharpest feedback this project has had:
better than Astro Gold technically, in line with Astro.com's register (Liz Greene: colloquial,
substantive, zero astrologese, never pop), in line with Sonia's method (domains, testimonies,
receipts, transits last). Every screen answers a person's real question, in their language,
with the working one layer down. And the daily check answers exactly two things:
**what is working with me today, and what is working against me.**
